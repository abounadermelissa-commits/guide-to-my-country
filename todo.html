Things To Do

"Nature doesn't explain. It shows."
Adventure:
Chamonix Via Ferrata
Cliffside routes cabled into the rock, climbing higher with each ridge. Mont Blanc rises in front of you the whole way up. It's slow, careful work — one hold at a time.
Verdon Gorge Kayaking
A canyon carved by the river over centuries. You paddle through it, walls rising on both sides. The water sets the pace. You just stay with it
Pyrenees Paragliding
A short run off a ridge near Ariège, then just air. The descent is long and quiet. You see the mountains in a way no trail can show you.
With Family:
Château de Chenonceau
A castle built across a river, with gardens made for walking, not just looking at. Kids can run free while everyone else takes in the history.
Disneyland Paris
Rides, parades, characters — a day built entirely around them. No history to take in, no pace to match. Just fun, start to finish.
Relaxing:
Vichy Thermal Baths
A town shaped around its mineral waters. The day slows down to match them. Nothing to plan. Just heat, water, and time passing.
Provence Lavender Fields
Fields turn violet every July, stretching out under open sky. There's little to do here but notice the color. Best with nowhere else to be.
